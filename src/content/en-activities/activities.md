activities:
  - title: "Climb the Dolomites"
    image: "/images/activities/Guida-Alpina-Bergfuehrer-plakat-FERRATE-02.avif"
    alt: "A climber on a via ferrata route in the Dolomites"
    phone: "+39 328 3583653"
    website: "https://www.discoverydolomites.com"
    content: |
      Try the unique experience of **via ferrata climbing** with the Alpine Guides of Bolzano. Discover the Dolomites with personalized tours including technical equipment and transfers from/to Villa Anita.
  - title: "Scenic flights & Elitaxi"
    image: "/images/activities/3-elicampiglio-ecureuil-as-350-1631275767_02080.avif"
    alt: "Helicopter flying over the Alps"
    phone: "+39 347 0608297"
    website: "https://www.elicampiglio.it"
    content: |
      **Elicampiglio** offers unforgettable panoramic flights and helitaxi transfers throughout the territory. Experience a new point of view over the Alps with expert pilots and high-end service.
  - title: "Museo Archeologico Ötzi"
    image: "/images/activities/08102015-_MG_5960-scaled.avif"
    alt: "Entrance of the South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology"
    phone: "+39 0471 320100"
    website: "https://www.iceman.it"
    content: |
      Since 1998, the museum is home to the famous mummy **Ötzi, the Iceman**. See the 5,300-year-old original mummy, his clothing, and equipment, alongside the latest archaeological research.
  - title: "Sant’Osvaldo walk"
    image: "/images/activities/BOrunning-77-scaled.avif"
    alt: "Panoramic view of Bolzano from the Sant'Osvaldo promenade"
    content: |
      Connecting Sant’Antonio with Santa Maddalena, this walk offers spectacular views of the **Rosengarten** massif. It is full of Mediterranean plants and vineyards where native wine is produced.
  - title: "Guncina walk"
    image: "/images/activities/BOrunning-133-scaled.avif"
    alt: "Lush vegetation along the historic Guncina path"
    content: |
      Prepared since 1892, this route leads from the **Gries district** through the Rio Fago gorge. It offers a gentle descent back to Bolzano with beautiful views along the Lungo Talvera.
  - title: "Christmas market"
    image: "/images/activities/LR-Xmas-_Y0A9934.avif"
    alt: "Wooden stalls at the Bolzano Christmas Market"
    website: "www.mercatinodinatalebz.it"
    content: |
      Experience the scent of cinnamon, spices, and mountain wood. The **Bolzano Christmas Market** is the most awaited event of the year, featuring traditional loden and a festive atmosphere.
  - title: "Gardens of the Talvera"
    image: "/images/activities/HR-spring-_Y0A5008-scaled.avif"
    alt: "Green meadows and trees lining the Talvera stream"
    content: |
      The **green lung** of the city. These lush meadows along the Talvera stream feature playgrounds, sports fields, and cycle paths. It is the ideal spot for relaxation or a circular stroll.
  - title: "Museion"
    image: "/images/activities/LR-facciata-multimediale-_Y0A9305.avif"
    alt: "The modern glass architecture of the Museion museum"
    phone: "+39 0471 223413"
    website: "https://www.museion.it"
    content: |
      A center for **contemporary culture** and art. The museum exhibits modern art, film, and performance, serving as a point of reference for local and international art.
  - title: "Renon cableway"
    image: "/images/activities/rittner-seilbahn_1673607516.avif"
    alt: "Red cable car ascending toward the Renon plateau"
    website: "https://www.renon.com"
    content: |
      Connect Bolzano to Soprabolzano in minutes. From the top, take the **historic Renon train** across vast meadows and fields for breathtaking glimpses of the Dolomites.
