### APARTMENT R-11 WITH BALCONY 🏡🌿

**(45 sqm · 1 to 4 guests + 1)**

📍 **Fourth floor with elevator – Via Renon 11, Bolzano city centre**

This **fourth-floor apartment** is in a **prime location**, just **100 m from the Renon cable car** and steps from the old town, opposite the train station. Ideal for **1–4 guests (+1)**.

🛏️ The apartment features a **double bedroom** and a **living room with sofa bed** (2 single beds) for additional guests.

🍳 The **fully equipped kitchen** offers hob, dishwasher, coffee machine, electric kettle, fridge and all necessary cooking utensils.

🚿 The **private bathroom** is equipped with shower, bidet and hairdryer.

🌿 The **balcony with city view** is perfect for relaxing after a day of exploration.

🎫 **Bolzano Bozen Card Mobility included!** – Enjoy free public transport during your stay.

🔑 **Self check-in with video**. Parking available at Villa Anita (Via Castel Roncolo 16) for €10/night.
