### B3 — ROMANTIC DOUBLE SUITE WITH SHOWER 💕🚿

**(24 sqm · 1 to 2 people + 1)**

📍 **Second floor (no elevator)**

This **romantic double suite** is located on the **second floor of Villa Anita** and is ideal for **1–2 people (+1)**, perfect for couples looking for a special and romantic accommodation in Bolzano.

🛏️ The suite features a **double bed** and an additional **sofa bed** for an extra guest, providing flexibility and comfort.

🚿 The special feature of this suite is the **shower directly in the room** as well as an additional **private bathroom** with WC, bidet and hairdryer – a unique setup for maximum comfort.

🌳 Enjoy the **view of the private garden** and relax in the romantic atmosphere of this special accommodation.

☕ Guests have access to a **common lounge with refreshment corner** equipped with coffee machine, kettle, microwave and fridge, as well as a **shared garden** with outdoor dining area and furniture.

The **independent entrance**, the **unique setup** and all essential comforts guarantee a romantic and unforgettable stay, just a few minutes from the center of Bolzano.
