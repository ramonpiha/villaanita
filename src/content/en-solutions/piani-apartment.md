### PIANI APARTMENT WITH PRIVATE GARDEN 🏡🌿

**(80 sqm · 1 to 6 guests)**

📍 **First floor with elevator – 5 min walk from the pedestrian area, Bolzano**

This **elegant first-floor apartment** is ideal for **1–6 guests** and perfect for temporary stays for work or study, just a 5-minute walk from the pedestrian area.

🛏️ The apartment features **2 bedrooms** (2 double bedrooms) and a **living room with sofa bed** for additional guests.

🍳 The **fully equipped kitchen** offers hob, dishwasher, microwave, fridge and all necessary cooking utensils.

🚿 The **bathroom** is equipped with shower, bidet and hairdryer.

🌿 The **private garden** with outdoor dining area and furniture is perfect for relaxing.

♿ The apartment is **wheelchair accessible** and features **floor heating** and **soundproof windows** for maximum comfort.

🔑 **Independent entrance**. Ideal for longer stays with washing machine and all necessary amenities.
