### 2 — STANDARD ROOM WITH SHARED MODERN BATHROOM 🛏️🌿
**(22 sqm · up to 3 guests)**

📍 **Upper floor (no elevator)**

This cozy and comfortable standard room is ideal for **couples, friends, or small families** looking for a **practical and budget-friendly stay in Bolzano**. With a size of **22 sqm**, the room features a double bed and a sofa bed, offering a warm and functional space.

🚿 Guests have access to **two shared modern bathrooms**, located **just outside the room on the same floor**. The bathrooms have been recently renovated and are equipped with a shower, heating, bidet, and hairdryer.

🌿 Guests can also enjoy the **shared garden** and the **lounge area**, perfect for relaxing moments. Villa Anita is located **just a few minutes from Bolzano’s city center** and close to **Castel Roncolo**, offering a quiet yet well-connected location.
