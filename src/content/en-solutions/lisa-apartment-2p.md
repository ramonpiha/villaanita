### APARTMENT LISA 2P — PIAZZA ERBE 🏡

**(50 sqm · 1 to 4 guests + 1)**

📍 **Second floor (no elevator) – Via dei Portici 71, Pedestrian old town**

This **apartment on the second floor of a historic building** is located **in the heart of Bolzano's pedestrian center** and is ideal for **1–4 guests (+1)**, perfect for temporary stays, freelancers and companies.

🛏️ The apartment offers **up to 4 sleeping places** with a **spacious living room with sofa bed**.

🍳 The **fully equipped kitchen** offers all necessary utensils, coffee machine, fridge and microwave oven.

🚿 The **private bathroom** is equipped with shower, bidet and hairdryer.

🪟 The **spacious living room with three windows overlooking Via Argentieri** offers brightness and views of the old town.

🎫 **Bolzano Bozen Card Mobility included!** – Enjoy free public transport during your stay.

🔑 Check-in at Villa Anita (Via Castel Roncolo 16). Video check-in available.
