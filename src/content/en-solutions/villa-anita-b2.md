### B2 — ATTIC DOUBLE ROOM 🛏️✨

**(12 sqm · 1 to 2 people)**

📍 **Second floor (no elevator)**

This **cozy attic double room** is located on the **second floor of Villa Anita** and is ideal for **1–2 people**, perfect for couples or solo travelers looking for an intimate and comfortable accommodation in Bolzano.

🛏️ The room features a **double bed** and charming attic architecture that creates a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

🚿 The **private bathroom with shower** is conveniently located **in the room** and is equipped with bidet and hairdryer, ensuring maximum comfort and privacy.

📺 Enjoy modern amenities including **Smart TV**, **free Wi-Fi**, and **underfloor heating** for year-round comfort.

☕ Guests have access to a **common lounge with refreshment corner** equipped with coffee machine, kettle, microwave, and fridge, as well as a **shared garden** with outdoor dining area and furniture.

The **garden view**, **city map availability**, and all essential comforts guarantee a practical and relaxing stay in this **entirely non-smoking property**, just a few minutes from the center of Bolzano.
