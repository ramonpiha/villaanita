### 105 — MARTIN DOUBLE ROOM 🛏️

**(22 sqm · 1 to 3 guests + 1)**

📍 **First floor (no elevator) – Via Brennero 4**

This **spacious double room** is located on the **first floor of a historic building**, just steps from the pedestrian old town and the Renon cable car.

🛏️ The room features a **double bed** plus an **extra single bed** for a third person. Children's cots available upon request.

🚿 The highlight is the **large modern private bathroom** with bath or shower, heating, hairdryer and complimentary bath products.

☕ The room offers a complete **coffee corner** with coffee machine (pods included), kettle, microwave and fridge.

🔑 **24h access with code**. Check-in at Villa Anita (Via Castel Roncolo 16), parking available (€10/night). **Adjoining rooms** available upon request.
