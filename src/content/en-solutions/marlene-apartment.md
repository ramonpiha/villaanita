**(75 sqm · 1 to 4 guests + 1)**

📍 **Ground floor – Via Leonardo da Vinci 14, Bolzano city centre**

🚿 The apartment provides **2 modern bathrooms** with shower, bidet, hairdryer and soundproof windows for maximum peace.

🌿 The **private furnished garden** with outdoor dining area is perfect for relaxing moments in the heart of the city.

🎫 **Bolzano Bozen Card Mobility included!** – Enjoy free public transport during your stay.
