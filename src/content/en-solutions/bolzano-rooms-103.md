### 103 — MARLEN DOUBLE ROOM 🛏️

**(16 sqm · 1 to 2 guests)**

📍 **Ground floor – Via Brennero 4**

This **cozy and modern double room** is located on the **ground floor of Bolzano Rooms** and is ideal for **1–2 guests**, perfect for couples or friends seeking comfort and convenience in Bolzano.

🛏️ The room, called **Marlen**, features a **double bed or twin beds** (depending on availability and preference) and a **Smart TV** for your entertainment.

🚿 The **private en-suite bathroom** is located directly in the room and is equipped with shower, heating and hairdryer, ensuring maximum comfort and privacy.

☕ The room offers a complete **coffee corner** with coffee machine (coffee pods included), electric kettle, microwave and fridge – everything you need for an independent stay.

🔑 Thanks to **24h access with code**, you can arrive at your convenience. **Check-in** takes place at Villa Anita (Via Castel Roncolo 16), where **parking is also available for €10/night**. Alternatively, **self check-in with codes** sent before arrival is possible.

The **ground floor** location ensures easy access. This **non-smoking property** with all essential comforts guarantees a comfortable stay, just a few minutes from the center of Bolzano.
