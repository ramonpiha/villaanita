### 5 — DOUBLE ROOM WITH BALCONY AND SHARED BATHROOMS 🛏️🌿

**(24 sqm · 1 to 3 people + 1)**

📍 **First floor (no elevator)**

This **spacious double room with balcony** is located on the **first floor of Villa Anita** and is ideal for **1–3 people (+1)**, perfect for couples, friends or small families looking for comfortable accommodation with private outdoor space in Bolzano.

🌿 The room features a **balcony with garden view**, ideal for relaxing outdoors. Inside you will find an additional **sofa bed**, **flat-screen Smart TV** and a **dining table**.

🚿 Guests have access to **two shared bathrooms**, located **outside the room along the corridor, on the same floor**. The bathrooms have been **recently renovated** and are equipped with shower, bidet, heating and hairdryer.

🍽️ The room also has a **complete kitchenette** with basic utensils, fridge, coffee machine and electric kettle, making it suitable for longer stays as well.

The **independent entrance**, the **outdoor dining area** with table and chairs in the garden, and the essential comforts guarantee a practical and relaxing stay, just a few minutes from the center of Bolzano.
