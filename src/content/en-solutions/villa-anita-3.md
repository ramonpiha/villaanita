### 3 — STANDARD ROOM WITH SHARED BATHROOM 🛏️
**(16 sqm · up to 3 guests)**

📍 **First floor (no elevator)**

This standard double room is located on the **first floor of Villa Anita** and offers a comfortable and functional space for **1 to 3 guests**. It is ideal for couples, friends, or small families looking for a simple and practical stay in Bolzano.

🚿 Guests have access to **two shared bathrooms**, located **just outside the room along the corridor on the same floor**. The bathrooms have been **recently renovated** and are equipped with a shower, heating, and hairdryer.

🍽️ The room also features a **small kitchenette** with basic cooking utensils and a fridge, making it suitable for longer stays. With independent access and essential comforts, it offers a relaxed and convenient accommodation close to the city.
