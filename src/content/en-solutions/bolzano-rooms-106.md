### 106 — RAUL TRIPLE ROOM 🛏️🌳

**(28 sqm · 1 to 3 guests + 1)**

📍 **First floor (no elevator) – Via Brennero 4**

This **spacious triple room** is located on the **first floor of a historic building**, ideal for families or friends, close to the old town and the Renon cable car.

🛏️ The room features a **double bed + 1 single bed** (or **3 single beds**). Children's cots available upon request.

🌳 The room enjoys a pleasant **view of the inner garden** and is equipped with mosquito net and fan.

🚿 Guests have access to a **shared bathroom on the floor**, equipped with shower, heating and hairdryer.

☕ The room offers a complete **coffee corner** and access to the **common lounge** with refreshment area.

🔑 **24h access with code**. Check-in at Villa Anita (Via Castel Roncolo 16), parking available (€10/night). **Adjoining rooms** available upon request.
