**(35 sqm · 1 to 4 guests + 1)**

📍 **First floor (no elevator) – Via Brennero 4**

☕ The room offers a complete **coffee corner** and access to the **common lounge**.

🔑 **24h access with code**. Check-in at Villa Anita (Via Castel Roncolo 16), parking available (€10/night).
