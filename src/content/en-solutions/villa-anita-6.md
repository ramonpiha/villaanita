### 6 — DOUBLE ROOM WITH SHARED BATHROOMS 🛏️

**(14 sqm · 1 to 2 people + 1)**

📍 **First floor (no elevator)**

This **cozy double room** is located on the **first floor of Villa Anita** and is ideal for **1–2 people (+1)**, perfect for couples or solo travelers looking for simple and functional accommodation in Bolzano.

🚿 Guests have access to **two shared bathrooms**, located **outside the room along the corridor, on the same floor**. The bathrooms have been **recently renovated** and are equipped with shower, bidet, heating and hairdryer – modern, clean and comfortable.

🍽️ The room also features a **kitchenette** with basic utensils, fridge, coffee machine and electric kettle, as well as a **dining table** and an additional **sofa bed**, making it suitable for longer stays as well.

The **independent entrance**, the **outdoor dining area** with table and chairs in the garden with garden view, and the essential comforts guarantee a practical and relaxing stay, just a few minutes from the center of Bolzano.
