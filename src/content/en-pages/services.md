## Bike Rent & Cycling
Explore Bolzano and its surroundings on two wheels.

### Bike Rental
* **Bike Sharing:** Ideal for getting around the city.
* **Bike South Tyrol – Südtirol Rad:** [www.suedtirol-rad.com](https://www.suedtirol-rad.com) – View the full rental list and locations.

### Popular Routes around Bolzano
* **Adige Cycle Path:** Through Val Venosta along the Via Claudia Augusta.
* **Bolzano – Brenner:** Along the Valle Isarco.
* **Wine Route Cycle Path:** A scenic route through local vineyards.

In each of our properties, you will find **city maps** with useful suggestions, historical itineraries, and cultural highlights.
