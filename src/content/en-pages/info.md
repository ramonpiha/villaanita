## 🏨 Check-In

The reception office is located at **Villa Anita, Via Castel Roncolo n. 16**.

**Hours: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM**

Some properties offer a **self check-in** service: in this case, you will receive all necessary information and codes to access the property independently.

## 📍 How to reach us

### 🚗 By Car
Villa Anita is located at **Via Castel Roncolo 16**, near the pedestrian zone. It is possible to reach the reception directly **without crossing restricted traffic areas (ZTL)**.

### 🚂 By Train
The train station is in the center, a few steps from the pedestrian area. **Villa Anita is 950 m away.** We recommend reaching our office on foot or by taxi (Phone: +39 0471 981111).

### 🚌 By Bud
* **Central Bus Station:** Located on Rittner Strasse next to the main train station.
* **Tourist Bus:** If you reach Bolzano by tourist bus, the stop is near the **Bolzano Sud** railway station. The center can be reached from there by regional train, local bus, or taxi.
